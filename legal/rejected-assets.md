# Rejected Assets

## 2026-07-08

- Bulk import from `lorgan3/sorcerers`: rejected. The repository is GPL-3.0 and
  contains many third-party asset sources whose exact per-file licenses must be
  checked individually.
- Worms-branded, Team17-style, or other brand-derived assets: rejected for
  product use unless separately cleared by the rights holder.
- NonCommercial, personal-use-only, no-redistribution, unclear, GPL/AGPL/LGPL,
  and CC-BY-SA assets: rejected for the first commercial/mobile-safe product
  asset policy.
