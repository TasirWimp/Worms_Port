## Purpose

WP-015D2G reconstructs the complete D2A candidate history as a compact,
source-bound navigation chart before another mechanic is proposed. It treats
the candidates as relational landmarks rather than as independent knob trials
or a search for one compressed cause of initiative pressure.

The starting commitment is a bounded directional wager:

```text
current carrier + protected family + accumulated landmarks
  -> choose one plausible nearby direction
  -> take one bounded and reversible design step
  -> inspect its context-indexed wake
  -> strengthen, retain, demote, reopen, or return
  -> update the carrier before choosing again
```

A future probe may expose that a relation reduces, reorganizes, exchanges,
displaces, preserves, obscures, or leaves unresolved the current pressure. It
does not need to prove a sole cause. Returning removes an experimental rule
from the gameplay carrier but must retain its witness, scoped failure, residue,
and reopening condition in the navigation carrier.

## Navigation protocol for this review

### Carriers

- **Gameplay carrier:** one immutable D2A config and its deterministic traces.
- **Navigation carrier:** the cross-candidate chart, protected anchors,
  landmarks, route status, residue, and re-entry history in this document.
- **Authority carrier:** V4 TypeScript remains outside candidate mutation and
  is referenced only through the existing authority boundary.

### Directional probe record

A later bounded step must declare, before implementation:

1. current carrier and parent;
2. directional wager, without requiring a sole-cause claim;
3. at least one anchor, guide, warning, and relevant negative landmark;
4. one coherent relational intervention, which may involve more than one
   numeric field only when those fields form one rule meaning;
5. starting-carrier, policy, route-history, resource, response-horizon, and
   model-cut scope;
6. expected local relational change and the evidence that would make another
   step in that direction reasonable;
7. protected anchors and explicit permissible residue;
8. stop, demotion, return, and reopening conditions; and
9. the escalation condition for V4 instrumentation or human playtesting.

This precommitment prevents adaptive rationalization. It does not require the
result to be scalar or context-independent.

### Coarse-to-fine execution order

```text
one or two exact route witnesses
  -> production-spawn neighborhood and relevant legality boundaries
  -> fixed-policy and response-capable policy cuts
  -> full declared comparator sweep
  -> authority-side instrumentation or playtest when D2A residue dominates
```

Every expansion is triggered by the previous wake. No package attempts an
exhaustive sweep of every legal starting distance by default.

### Anchor landmarks

1. A visible commitment has a credible response at the decision horizon where
   the threatened outcome is still avoidable.
2. A response creates inspectable local reorganization through position,
   Stitching, bounded resource, action availability, or another declared
   carrier; a free non-terminal reset is blocked.
3. The declared policy witness converges without relying on a repeated
   support-complete carrier or hidden turn-limit progress.
4. Bounded forced openings, rejected actions, obligations, and response timing
   remain visible rather than disappearing behind aggregate outcomes.
5. Initiative and route viability remain indexed by starting carrier, actor,
   policy pairing, resource state, and path history; no universal numeric
   balance threshold is inferred.
6. Threadball, Needlepoint, and Spoolburst retain distinguishable range,
   damage, commitment, and response roles unless a later owner decision
   explicitly changes that product direction.
7. Production spawn authority stays at distance 640. Neighboring and remote
   distances are pressure witnesses, not alternate spawn commitments.
8. D2A remains an ideal-direct analytical cut. Terrain, aim, trajectory,
   splash, player skill, live Loomkeeper behavior, UI, replay, networking, and
   rewards remain explicit residue.

### Horizon landmarks

- readable and enjoyable mobile artillery play under authoritative terrain,
  aiming, timing, and human decision-making;
- robust tactical variety beyond the current transparent policy family; and
- a separately owner-approved TypeScript ruleset decision, if analytical and
  playtest evidence eventually support one.

These horizons orient navigation but cannot certify an analytical route.

## Phase 2 initial pressure-basin review

The reconstructed routes do not support compressing the surviving pressure
into one defect or one tuning dimension. They expose several coupled basins.
The basins are navigation groupings: they say where a probe wake must remain
visible, not what ultimately causes the gameplay result.

These basins interact rather than form a pipeline with a single root. A
typical observed route has the following coupled relation:

```text
starting carrier + accumulated path
  -> legal actions and active support
  -> policy-conditioned selection
  -> response horizon and residual
  -> later reachable carriers
  -> terminal, recurrent, or redirected route
```

Changing one relation can therefore reorganize several downstream surfaces
without the change being a general repair. This is why F3 can repair
recurrence while worsening initiative, H2 can improve aggregate parity while
preserving a distance split, and I2 can change cross-policy action selection
without changing any same-policy mirror.
