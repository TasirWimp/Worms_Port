## Strongest licensed claim

This package may test, inside the existing ideal-direct-hit D2A tactical model,
whether separating movement-created direct-cast legality into an exposed
relocation followed by an opponent normal turn removes the long-distance
initiative pressure retained by F4 without reintroducing forced openings,
recurrence, turn-limit outcomes, or hidden response residue.

It cannot establish live terrain/ballistics behavior, player fun, strategy
robustness, balance, V5 approval, or production authority. A useful analytical
result would only license later owner judgment and, if selected, a separate
TypeScript ruleset proposal.

### One-sentence hypothesis

F4's long-distance initiative pressure is partly carried by direct casts whose
legality is created by movement inside the same action; requiring that exact
range entry to resolve as a relocation, with an opponent normal turn before a
later cast, may create timely counterplay without changing values or adding a
defensive status.

This is a falsifiable hypothesis, not a causal conclusion.

## Exact candidate semantics

I1 adds one optional analytical rule to F4:

1. For a proposed ordinary direct `cast`, compute whether its Relic is legal at
   the authoritative tactical distance before movement.
2. Compute the normal post-movement distance using the existing 64-unit move,
   world bounds, Seam Pin, and Escape-Slack rules.
3. If the cast is illegal before movement but legal after that movement, omit
   that combined move-and-cast action from `legal_actions`.
4. Preserve the ordinary `relocate` action. If the actor chooses it, movement
   completes, the turn ends, and the opponent receives one normal turn under
   the existing rules and policies.
5. On the original actor's next normal turn, it may choose any then-legal
   action. It may cast only if the target is still in band. No cast is reserved,
   owed, guaranteed, or automatically resolved.

The legality band is inclusive. With the current zero minimum ranges, I1 acts
only when approaching from above a Relic's maximum range. A direct cast already
legal before movement remains an ordinary F4 move-and-cast option even if the
actor also moves. A move that still leaves every direct cast illegal remains an
ordinary relocation.

I1 deliberately does **not** change:

- stationary or already-in-band direct casts;
- `prepare_spoolburst`, including movement that first makes preparation legal;
- stationary prepared-Spoolburst release;
- Threadball Unweave and its compulsory Threadback;
- Seam Pin, Escape Slack, Cocoon, damage, ranges, movement size, world bounds,
  turn limit, policies, or opening-search depth; or
- any V1-V4 production state, command, event, replay, protocol, client, server,
  Loomkeeper, reward, UI, or asset behavior.

“Commitment” is an edge/path role: the actor has publicly spent its current
turn entering a direct-cast band. It is not a new timeless cut/node role,
status, meter, resource, reaction action, or persistent tactical-state field.
The full post-relocation tactical state and path history carry re-entry.

### Boundary domain

The complete primary comparison domain is:

- distances: `511, 512, 513, 575, 576, 577, 639, 640, 641, 703, 704, 705`;

This yields 1,200 primary matches per config and 4,800 across the four-way

The domain has four distinct readings:

- `511/512/513` verifies that prepared Spoolburst is not double-wrapped;
- `575/576/577` brackets direct Threadball range entry;

- `703/704/705` brackets the upper one-move Needlepoint entry boundary.

Distance 705 is a negative control only for **first-action** Needlepoint entry:
one 64-unit move reaches 641, still outside the 640 band. It is not a promised
whole-match no-effect control. Later paths may cross one or more bands and must
remain in the evidence.

### Targeted transition witnesses

In addition to the primary matrix, deterministic focused tests must construct:

1. at 704, an F4 `cast:needlepoint:<approach>` that is legal only after movement;
2. the same I1 state, where that cast is absent and the matching relocation is
   present;
3. at 640, an I1 Needlepoint cast that remains legal because the Relic was
   already in band before movement;
4. at 705, no first-action direct cast, confirming the upper boundary;
5. at 577 and 641, the corresponding Threadball and Needlepoint splits;
6. at 513, unchanged Spoolburst preparation behavior; and
7. a complete `entry relocation -> opponent normal response -> original actor
   later casts or cannot cast` route for each direct Relic that I1 affects.

- **response:** the opponent's ordinary next-turn action, not a special
  reaction;
- **evidence:** before/after carriers, legal-action sets or digests, edge and
  trace witnesses, reports, probes, and recurrence/return rows;
- **support:** all continuation fields above;
- **return:** transfer-back to exact config/model/report/trace steps; and
- **forbidden:** production mutation, dynamic code, hidden randomness,
  undeclared domains, protocol/replay/reward/UI/server/client/asset changes.

## Required edge and voyage anatomy

The rendered I1 voyage must distinguish:

1. `outside_direct_range -> entered_direct_range`
   - local edge kind: range-entry relocation;
   - motif: move;
   - no automatic CRPM primitive interpretation;
   - newly visible: which direct Relics became legal;
   - residue: spent turn, changed position/distance, movement/resource effects,
     opponent action ownership, and any other bands crossed.
2. `entered_direct_range -> opponent_normal_response`
   - local edge kind: ordinary opponent response;
   - response may cast, relocate, prepare, unweave, or otherwise use only
     existing legal actions;
   - the edge may remove, preserve, or reorganize the original cast route.
3. `post_response -> later_resolution_or_abandonment`
   - local edge kind: later cast resolution, alternative action, or unavailable
     cast;
   - a later cast is evidence only when it is newly declared and legal in that
     later carrier.

No `WorldObligation` is opened solely by I1: the actor receives no reserved
future cast and the opponent owes no special response. Path history must still
carry the entry initiator, affected Relic set, response edge, and terminal
classification. Existing F4 preparation/Cocoon/Threadback obligations remain
separate and must continue to open, carry, discharge, or expire correctly.

The return evaluation keeps visible equality, protected equivalence, recursive
carrier return, invariant-region membership, finite exact return, and route
mismatch distinct. A repeated position is not recurrence when actor, reserve,
status, turn, or path support differs.

## Intentionally forgotten distinctions and excluded claims

The D2A cut intentionally excludes terrain cells, collision, aim/power,
trajectory, splash, projectile timing, animation, information latency, player
skill, live Loomkeeper behavior, network/session state, replay serialization,
UI, rewards, and assets. Those distinctions are not declared irrelevant; they
remain explicit model residue.

I1 therefore excludes claims of:

- real ballistic cast legality or evasion;
- player-visible clarity, responsiveness, or fun;
- optimal or human strategy;
- global balance or convergence;
- sufficiency outside the twelve-distance, policy, seed, and horizon domain;
- unique/minimal carrier support;
- full CRPM relation, ontology, or graph-safe schema;
- V5 readiness or any production activation.

## Mandatory probes

### Structural probes

- forced opening actions by distance, orientation, and Relic;
- non-terminal recurrence count and exact first witness;
- turn-limit and other terminal results;
- direct-cast legality before and after every movement;
- suppressed movement-created casts by Relic/distance/policy;
- executed range-entry relocations by Relic made newly legal;
- opponent response actions immediately after entry;
- later direct-cast resolutions, abandoned routes, and routes made illegal by
  the response;
- F4 preparation, Cocoon absorption, Unweave, and Threadback counts;
- Escape-Slack expenditure and movement residue;
- exact parent/comparator report and trace regressions; and
- quotient-transport/alias witnesses for any proposed reduced range-entry cut.

### Scalar probes kept separate

- first-actor rate in aggregate, by distance, orientation, and policy pairing;
- average turns and terminal cause by distance;
- action and Relic frequencies;
- damage and resource use;
- range-entry use and later-resolution rates; and
- comparator deltas computed only over the identical expanded domain.

No scalar threshold automatically produces acceptance. In particular, a
better aggregate cannot hide a boundary, orientation, policy, timing, or
support split.

## Falsifiers and disposition rules

### Cheapest discriminating tests

Before a full sweep, fail the implementation if:

- the I1 704 state still exposes the movement-created direct Needlepoint cast;
- the matching relocation is unavailable;
- the in-band 640 direct Needlepoint cast is removed;
- the 513 Spoolburst preparation behavior changes;
- F4 without I1 changes behavior or report digest; or
- a path renderer calls the entry itself a completed cast or special response.

### Hard structural gates

I1 is rejected if any primary match in the declared domain:

- has a bounded forced opening that the F4 comparator does not have;
- records a non-terminal tactical recurrence;
- reaches the analytical turn limit;
- loses an existing F4 preparation/Cocoon/Unweave/Threadback protected member
  through an I1 implementation error;
- hides a live existing obligation or response edge; or
- advances a later cast without an intervening opponent normal action after the
  movement-created entry.

I1 is also rejected as dead configuration if the focused witnesses pass but no
primary route executes an affected range-entry relocation.

### Analytical disposition

If the hard gates pass, evaluation may assign only:

- `structural_reference` when I1 supplies timely path reorganization but
  initiative, duration, policy, or boundary residue remains material;
- `accepted_for_analysis` when all declared pressure cases pass and the owner
  judges the non-scalar profile useful enough to retain as a future comparator;
  or
- `residualized` when the model cannot decide the question because an excluded
  port is outcome-relevant.

The owner must judge whether route diversity, initiative distribution,
response timing, match duration, and affected-Relic use form a useful gameplay
shape. There is no generic balanced-rate interval and no automatic promotion.
Every disposition leaves ProductAuthority `none`.

## Evidence covariance and deduplication

C4, F4, H2, and I1 share the same D2A model, ideal-hit assumption, policy
family, scenario construction, deterministic seed, and much of their config
lineage. Their rows are counterfactual comparisons, not independent empirical
experiments. Deduplicate by source/model/config/domain/orientation/policy/seed/
trace/cut/adapter/evaluation identity.

Boundary rows, mirrors, policies, scalar probes, reruns, adapters, renderings,
and reviewer agreement improve coverage but do not multiply empirical weight.
No result is player-behavior or live-ballistics evidence.
