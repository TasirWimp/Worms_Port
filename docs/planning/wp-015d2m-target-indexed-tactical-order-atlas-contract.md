## Purpose

D2M tests whether the locally valid F4 preparation-response relations already
observed by D2I, D2K, and D2L assemble into a role-neutral route-level tactical
order. It does not assume that one actor, action, payoff, distance, or scalar is
the cause of the remaining initiative pressure.

The bounded question is:

> Do local Cocoon formation, response, resolution, and reopening paths compose
> into the declared protected tactical order; where they do not, which
> distance-, phase-, policy-, route-, support-, or authority-indexed residue
> obstructs that assembly?

The prior phrase `response-edge ownership map` is retained only as an edge
annotation. Actor identity and responder phase are coordinates of a local
route, not a global owner, carrier ranking, or sufficient explanation.

## Protected tactical-order family v0

The following targets are separate judgments, in this exact order:

1. `local_answerability`
   - a visible preparation carrier exposes legal responses before resolution;
   - multiplicity is a relation, not a forced unique action.
2. `local_reorganization`
   - responses create inspectably different support, position, damage,
     resource, status, or continuation paths rather than a free reset.
3. `ordered_composition`
   - formation, response, intervening action, release, and reopening compose
     coherently across the declared overlaps without hiding path order.
4. `recursive_terminal_closure`
   - declared continuations finish without nonterminal support-complete
     recurrence or hidden turn-limit progress.
5. `actor_role_transport`
   - actor/mirror transport does not turn the same local response relation into
     an undeclared phase privilege; failures remain route-indexed.
6. `authority_reentry`
   - every compact chart and judgment retains enough source/result digest,
     carrier, response, route, and exclusion references to reconstruct the
     bounded evidence without claiming source-history recovery.

No averaging or majority vote combines these targets. A target may pass,
remain relational, fail descent, or be bounded-only while another target has a
different result.
