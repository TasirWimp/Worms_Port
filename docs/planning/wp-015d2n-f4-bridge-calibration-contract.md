does not descend to one complete continuation relation. D2N asks the narrower
pre-expansion question:

> Which distinctions already present in the richer F4 carrier separate those

finite-domain congruent without being causal, minimal, globally sufficient, or
appropriate gameplay state. The package must preserve covariant refinements as
a family when the present domain cannot distinguish their meanings.
