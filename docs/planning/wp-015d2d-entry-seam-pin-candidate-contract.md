## Failure-derived witness

F4 is structurally admissible in the declared twelve-distance frame but records
80 first-actor wins out of 100 at each of 641, 703, and 704. At each of those
distances:

- 40 matches begin with a movement-created Needlepoint cast;
- all 40 entry casts deal 30 damage;
- all 40 apply a one-turn Seam Pin and caster cooldown; and
- all 40 are won by the first actor.

At 704, the matched F4 `range_pressure` route is:

```text
704 -> move and Needlepoint at 640 -> 30 damage + Seam Pin
    -> pinned opponent ordinary turn
```

I1 removes the complete combined cast, produces zero such entry attacks, and
changes the 641/703/704 rates to 32 percent while also creating four turn-limit
results at 705. That is evidence that a full-turn commitment removes too much
of the entry edge. It does not yet prove that Seam Pin alone causes the F4
initiative band; I2 is the bounded ablation that tests that hypothesis.

## Exact I2 semantics

I2 is an exact F4 child with one additional rule:

> When Needlepoint is outside its maximum range before its own movement and
> inside range after that movement, the combined move-and-cast remains legal
> and deals the normal 30 damage, but that hit does not apply Seam Pin and does
> not start the caster's Seam-Pin cooldown.

The suppression is edge-scoped to that one cast. It creates no persistent
state, right, resource, reaction, response action, damage modifier, or delayed
resolution.

Unchanged behavior includes:

- stationary Needlepoint and Needlepoint already legal before movement;
- F4's ordinary `advance_only` Seam-Pin rule on other eligible Needlepoint
  advances;
- Threadball and Spoolburst ranges, damage, and actions;
- Spoolburst preparation, stationary release, Spun Cocoon, Unweave, and
  Threadback;
- equal 128 Escape Slack, movement values, Stitching, policies, search depth,
  turn order, and recurrence key.

The entry test uses the authoritative pre-movement and post-movement tactical
carriers. It is not inferred from a rendered distance label after the fact.
