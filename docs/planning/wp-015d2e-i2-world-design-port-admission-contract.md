evidence without rewriting the sealed WP-015D2B registry, hiding the start-769
horizon residue, or promoting I2 into V5?

The owner keeps production spawn authority around distance 640. The admissible
analytical pressure frame is therefore the already reviewed twelve-distance

Distance 640 is the production-spawn authority reference. The other distances

## Authority and compatibility boundary

`shared/simulation.ts` remains the only production gameplay authority. This
package does not alter I2 semantics, the Python tactical transition order, any
V1-V4 behavior, or any live module.

WP-015D2B is a sealed historical-analysis carrier. Its implementation lock,
registered F2/F3/F4/H2/H3 adapter chain, request/result digests, and evidence
records must remain byte-identical and executable. D2E therefore adds a
parallel, versioned I2 admission layer that reuses stable D2B contracts and
canonical helpers but does not edit the sealed registry, executor, schemas,
implementation lock, CLI, or package scripts.

The D2E chain is:

```text
D2A schema-16 tactical authority at Worms_Port commit 9da87c9
  -> d2e_i2_analytical_export@1 deterministic candidate evidence
    -> d2e_i2_world_design@1 fail-closed request/result validation
      -> CRPM-world profile-v3 M2 analytical result
```

The final execution receipt must bind a committed D2E implementation tree and
per-path Git blobs. Working-tree execution may be tested before sealing, but it
cannot produce the final authenticated result or completed evidence record.

report, and excluded ports. A compact rendered edge is never claimed as the
full relation; it retains reversible report/match/trace references.

## Fail-closed conditions

Reject execution if:

- the config/schema/source blob, seed, distance set, policy set, first-actor or
  mirror domain, horizon, or report digest drifts;
- any requested field is ignored or any unknown request/result field appears;
- a dynamic module, arbitrary operator, shell command, live activation, V5,
  protocol, replay, reward, wallet, client, server, networking, asset, or
  production mutation is requested;
- the required matched entry, next-response, already-in-band control, or 769
  warning witness cannot be reconstructed;
- comparator digests differ from their D2D bindings;
- the D2B sealed suite or lock changes; or
- the final result lacks a committed implementation receipt.

## Evaluation and maturity

The diagnostic profile must retain:

- mixed route pressure rather than aggregate-only success;
- explicit pin/cooldown, damage, position, response, policy, terminal, and
  unmodelled residue;
- partial local reorganization: I2 changes affected routes but neither proves

  production decision.

ends at `M2_local_use`, analytical disposition `structural_reference`, and
ProductAuthority `none`.
